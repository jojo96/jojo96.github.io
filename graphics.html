Theatre

  • Performed in Yerma, Yerma, Yerma, a contemporary reinterpretation of Federico García Lorca’s classic, exploring themes of social pressure, identity, and belonging through a diverse international cast.
  • Participated in The Wonderlaanstraatweg, where seventeen non-native speakers created and performed original scenes inspired by personal experiences in Flanders and the fantasy world of Alice in Wonderland.
